# Post-Event Follow-Up Sequences

## The 48-Hour Sprint

The first 48 hours after an event determine whether your leads turn into pipeline or go cold. Every day you wait, response rates drop.

**Day 1 — Within 24 hours of landing:**
1. All leads uploaded to CRM with campaign membership and scores
2. All leads assigned to an owner
3. ALL score 4 leads have received a personal email from their assigned AE
4. LinkedIn connection requests sent to all score 3-4 leads

**Day 2 — Within 48 hours:**
1. ALL score 3 leads have received a personalized email from their assigned BDR
2. Score 1-2 leads enrolled in MAP nurture sequence
3. All promises fulfilled (case studies sent, intros made, links shared)
4. CRM notes updated with conversation context for all score 3-4 leads

**Non-negotiable rule:** 100% of score 3-4 leads must have a personal first touch within 48 hours. Not 80%. Not "most of them." All of them. This is the single highest-leverage activity in your entire event ROI.

## Hot Lead Sequence (Score 4) — 3 Touches Over 7 Days

### Touch 1 — Day 1 (within 24 hours)

**Sent by:** AE who will own the deal (or who had the conversation)

**Subject options:**
- `Great connecting at {Event} — next steps`
- `{Event} follow-up — {specific topic you discussed}`
- `As promised — {thing you said you'd send}`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

Really enjoyed our conversation at {Event} — especially your perspective on {specific thing they said or cared about}.

{If you promised something: "As promised, here's [case study / link / intro / pricing info]."}

Based on what you shared about {their situation}, I think there's a strong fit. I'd love to show you how {Customer} solved a similar challenge — they went from {before} to {after} within {timeframe}.

Would {Day} at {Time} work for a 25-minute call? I can walk you through exactly how it would work for {their company}.

{Sender}
{Title} | {Company}
{Phone}
```

### Touch 2 — Day 3

**Subject:** `{Name}, one more thing from our {Event} conversation`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

Wanted to share something I thought you'd find relevant — {link to case study, blog post, or guide that directly relates to their challenge}.

{One sentence about why this is relevant to what they told you.}

Still hoping to connect this week. {Same calendar link or time proposal.}

{Sender}
```

### Touch 3 — Day 7

**Subject:** `Quick check-in — {Event} follow-up`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

Wanted to check in after {Event}. I know the post-conference inbox avalanche is real.

{New angle: share a different proof point, a relevant trend, or an insight from another conversation at the event.}

Would love to get 20 minutes on your calendar before {end of month / specific date}. Here's my availability: {link}

If the timing isn't right, no pressure — just let me know and I'll follow up when it makes more sense.

{Sender}
```

## Warm Lead Sequence (Score 3) — 5 Touches Over 14 Days

### Touch 1 — Day 1-2 (within 48 hours)

**Sent by:** BDR assigned to the account

**Subject options:**
- `{Event} follow-up — {topic or challenge}`
- `Connecting from {Event}`
- `Enjoyed our chat at {Event}`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

Good meeting you at {Event}. I caught our conversation about {topic} — it's something we're hearing a lot from {their role/industry} teams.

Thought you'd find this useful: {link to relevant content — guide, report, or blog post}.

If you'd like to explore how we're helping teams like yours with {outcome}, I'm happy to set up a quick call. Here's my calendar: {link}

{Sender}
{Title} | {Company}
```

### Touch 2 — Day 4

**Subject:** `{Resource name} — thought of you`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

Following up from {Event} — wanted to share {specific resource: case study, ROI calculator, or guide}.

{One sentence about why: "Given what you mentioned about [challenge], I thought this would be useful."}

Worth a quick look. Happy to walk through it if you'd like context.

{Sender}
```

### Touch 3 — Day 7

**Subject:** `How {Customer} solved {their problem}`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

Quick story I think you'll relate to — {Customer}, a {industry/size similar to theirs} company, was dealing with {same problem the prospect described}.

Within {timeframe}, they {specific outcome with metric}.

If that sounds like something worth exploring for {their company}, let's find 20 minutes. {Calendar link}

{Sender}
```

### Touch 4 — Day 10

**Subject:** `Quick question about {their challenge}`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

One quick question — {specific question about their situation based on what you learned at the event}.

Asking because we've seen {industry} teams approach this a few different ways, and the right answer usually depends on {variable relevant to them}.

Either way, would love to hear your take. {Calendar link if they want to discuss live.}

{Sender}
```

### Touch 5 — Day 14

**Subject:** `Should I close the loop?`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

I've reached out a few times since {Event} — want to make sure I'm being helpful, not annoying.

If {challenge you discussed} isn't a priority right now, totally understood. I'll check back in a couple months.

If it is, I've got a few ideas I think would be worth 15 minutes of your time. Just reply "yes" and I'll send a calendar link.

{Sender}
```

## Cold Lead Sequence (Score 1-2) — 3 Automated Touches Over 21 Days

These go through the MAP, not sent manually.

### Touch 1 — Day 3

**Subject:** `{Event} recap — what we took away`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

Thanks for stopping by our booth at {Event}!

Here are three things we took away from the event:
1. {Key theme or trend relevant to attendees}
2. {Insight about the industry}
3. {Observation about a common challenge}

We're helping {industry} teams with {high-level outcome}. If you're curious, here's a quick overview: {link to product page or overview content}.

{Company} Team
```

### Touch 2 — Day 10

**Subject:** `{Content piece title}`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

After {Event}, we pulled together {content piece — guide, report, webinar replay} on {topic relevant to the event theme}.

{One sentence about what they'll learn / why it matters.}

Check it out here: {link}

{Company} Team
```

### Touch 3 — Day 21

**Subject:** `One more from {Event}`

**Template:**
```
Hi {Name},

Last note from us after {Event}. We're hosting a {webinar / workshop / live demo} on {topic} on {date}.

{One sentence about what attendees will get out of it.}

Register here: {link}

If now's not the right time, no worries — we'll keep sharing useful content in your inbox.

{Company} Team
```

## LinkedIn Follow-Up Protocol

**For all score 3-4 leads — same day as email:**

Connection request (if not already connected):
```
Great meeting you at {Event}, {Name}. Enjoyed our conversation about {topic}. Looking forward to connecting here too.
```

**Day 3-5 (after connecting):**
Like or comment on one of their recent posts. Don't pitch — just engage genuinely.

**Day 7-10 (if email sequence isn't getting responses):**
Send a LinkedIn message as an alternative channel:
```
Hi {Name} — dropped you an email after {Event} but wanted to try you here too. {One sentence value prop}. Worth a quick chat? {Calendar link}
```
