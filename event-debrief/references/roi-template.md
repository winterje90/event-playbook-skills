# 30-Day Event ROI Report Framework

## When to Run This Report

- **Day 5:** Preliminary numbers for the team debrief (leads, scores, meetings booked)
- **Day 14:** Mid-month check (pipeline created, follow-up compliance)
- **Day 30:** Full ROI report (pipeline attributed, cost analysis, go/no-go for next year)
- **Day 60-90:** Optional update (closed-won attribution, if your sales cycle allows)

### Section 4: ROI Calculations

| Metric | Formula | Value |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Cost per lead (all) | Total cost ÷ Total leads | |
| Cost per qualified lead (Score 3+) | Total cost ÷ Score 3+ leads | |
| Cost per meeting booked | Total cost ÷ Total meetings | |
| Cost per opportunity | Total cost ÷ Opportunities created | |
| **Pipeline-to-cost ratio** | **Pipeline generated ÷ Total cost** | **Target: 10x+** |
| Revenue-to-cost ratio (at 90 days) | Closed-won revenue ÷ Total cost | Track at 90 days |

**Interpretation:**

| Pipeline-to-Cost Ratio | Assessment | Action |
|------------------------|-----------|--------|
| 10x+ | Strong performer | Repeat and invest more next year |
| 5-10x | Solid return | Repeat with optimizations |
| 3-5x | Marginal | Review execution — was this a planning issue or a fit issue? |
| Under 3x | Poor return | Either fundamentally fix the approach or reallocate budget |

**Important:** The pipeline-to-cost ratio is the *primary* metric, not the only metric. A 6x ratio with strong brand positioning and customer expansion might be more valuable than a 12x ratio from a niche event with no strategic angle.

## 5-Day Team Debrief Meeting Agenda

**When:** Within 5 business days of the event
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Who:** Everyone who attended + marketing lead + sales manager

### Agenda

**1. What Worked (10 min)**
- What generated the most conversations?
- Which outreach touches got the best response?
- What was the most effective booth engagement tactic?
- Did the talk track resonate?
- Side event feedback?

**2. What Didn't Work (10 min)**
- Where did we lose people?
- Was the booth messaging clear?
- Any logistics failures (WiFi, lead capture, booth setup)?
- What took too long or felt disorganized?

**3. Top 5 Conversations (15 min)**
Each team member shares their #1 conversation:
- Who was it? Company, role, size.
- What do they care about?
- What was the next step agreed upon?
- Has follow-up been sent?

**4. Surprise Leads (5 min)**
- Anyone NOT on the original TAL who turned out to be great?
- Any unexpected use cases or verticals?
- Any partner opportunities that emerged?

**5. Competitive Intelligence (5 min)**
- What were competitors saying?
- Any new product announcements or positioning changes?
- How did attendees react to their messaging?
- Anything we should adjust in our competitive positioning?

**6. One Change for Next Time (5 min)**
Each person names ONE thing they'd do differently:
- "More pre-event meetings"
- "Different booth location"
- "Better demo environment"
- "Shorter talk track"

**7. Preliminary Numbers (10 min)**
- Share the activity metrics from Section 2 (what's available by day 5)
- Pipeline created so far
- SLA compliance (% of leads with first touch sent)
- Budget actuals vs. planned (rough)

### Post-Debrief Action Items

- [ ] Document learnings in the event folder (`debrief.md`)
- [ ] Update CRM with any missing data
- [ ] Schedule 30-day ROI report review
- [ ] If event is recurring: preliminary go/no-go for next year based on early data

## Benchmarks for Comparison

Use these to contextualize your results:

| Metric | Below Average | Average | Above Average | Elite |
|--------|-------------|---------|--------------|-------|
| Pre-event meetings booked | Under 5 | 10-15 | 15-25 | 25+ |
| Qualified conversations (Score 3+) | Under 20 | 20-40 | 40-80 | 80+ |
| Follow-up within 48 hrs | Under 50% | 50-80% | 80-95% | 95-100% |
| Post-event meetings booked (% of Score 3+) | Under 10% | 10-20% | 20-30% | 30%+ |
| Pipeline-to-cost ratio | Under 3x | 3-5x | 5-10x | 10x+ |
| Cost per qualified lead | Over $500 | $200-$500 | $100-$200 | Under $100 |

**Context matters:** A $150K ACV company at a niche event will have different numbers than a $10K ACV company at a major conference. Compare against your own past events more than against industry benchmarks.

## Go/No-Go Framework for Next Year

At Day 30, answer these questions:

1. **Did the pipeline math work?** (Pipeline-to-cost ratio above 5x?)
2. **Was the ICP density high enough?** (Did we talk to the right people?)
3. **Did our team execute well?** (If not, was it fixable?)
4. **Was there strategic value beyond pipeline?** (Brand, partnerships, customer expansion)
5. **Could we do better next year with the same budget?** (What would we change?)

| Answers | Recommendation |
|---------|---------------|
| Yes to 1-2 + Yes to 3-4 | **Go.** Budget early, start planning 16 weeks out. |
| Mixed on 1-2, Yes to 3-4 | **Conditional Go.** Fix the execution issues, adjust budget. |
| No to 1-2, Yes to 3-4 | **Reconsider format.** Maybe a hosted dinner instead of a booth. |
| No across the board | **No.** Reallocate budget. |
