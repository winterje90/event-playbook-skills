# 12-Week Event Countdown Template

Use this framework to build a timeline working backwards from the event start date. Calculate actual dates for each milestone.

## Week 12-10: Foundation

| Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Goals and success metrics defined | Marketing Lead | Written goals doc with specific pipeline/meeting targets |
| Budget approved and allocated | Marketing Lead + Finance | Approved budget with line items |
| TAL building started | Marketing + Sales | Initial target account list criteria defined, data sources identified |
| Exhibitor manual requested and reviewed | Marketing | Manual obtained, key deadlines extracted, action items logged |
| Booth space and location confirmed | Marketing | Contract signed, booth number assigned |
| Insurance documents submitted | Marketing/Legal | Proof of insurance sent to organizer |

**If planning starts at Week 10 or later:** Compress W12-10 into the first week. Skip nothing — just do it faster. The most common failure mode is teams that skip goal-setting because they "don't have time" and then can't measure ROI.

## Week 8-6: Build Phase

| Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Outreach sequence #1 launched (Touch 1-2) | Sales/BDR | First 2 touches sent to TAL Tier 1 accounts |
| Marketing plan finalized | Marketing | Channels, content, timeline, owners documented |
| MOPS campaign created in CRM | MOPS/RevOps | Campaign, lead source, member statuses, scoring rules configured |
| MAP setup complete | MOPS/RevOps | Landing page live, UTM structure set, email templates loaded |
| Booth design brief sent to vendor | Marketing | Messaging, specs, graphics brief submitted |
| Swag ordered | Marketing | Items selected, quantities confirmed, delivery date set |
| Speaking slot content started | Speaker | Outline or draft in progress (if applicable) |
| Side event venue sourced | Marketing | 2-3 venue options identified, pricing confirmed (if applicable) |
| Customer attendees identified | CSM/Marketing | List of existing customers attending, shared with CS team |

## Week 5-4: Activation Phase

| Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Outreach sequence #2 (Touch 3-4) | Sales/BDR | Touches 3-4 sent, responses being tracked |
| TAL refined based on outreach engagement | Sales/BDR | Remove non-responders from Tier 1, promote engaged Tier 2 accounts |
| Side event invitations sent | Marketing | Guest list finalized, invites out (if applicable) |
| Booth design approved | Marketing | Final design signed off, sent to production |
| Additional collateral ordered | Marketing | Standees, brochures, one-pagers ordered |

## Week 3-2: Final Prep

| Milestone | Owner | Deliverable |
|-----------|-------|-------------|

## Milestone Status Tracking

When writing milestones to `status.md`, use these statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Not Started | Milestone is upcoming, no work has begun |
| In Progress | Work has started but deliverable isn't complete |
| Done | Deliverable is complete |
| At Risk | Behind schedule or blocked — needs attention |
| Skipped | Intentionally skipped (with reason noted) |

## Late Start Adjustments

If the team is starting with fewer than 12 weeks:

**8 weeks out:** Combine W12-10 foundation into a single "Week 0" sprint. Everything in that block gets done in the first 3-5 days. No shortcuts — just compressed.

**6 weeks out:** Cut Tier 2/3 outreach. Focus all effort on Tier 1 accounts (top 20-30). Reduce outreach from 6 touches to 4. Skip swag if it can't arrive in time — nobody noticed you didn't have swag, but they'll notice if you don't have meetings.

**4 weeks out:** Emergency mode. Goals, TAL Tier 1 only, 3-touch outreach, booth basics, team brief. Skip side events unless they're already in motion. Focus ruthlessly on pre-booked meetings — that's where your ROI will come from.

**2 weeks out:** You're mostly doing damage control. Focus exclusively on: (1) outreach to 10-15 must-meet accounts, (2) booth logistics, (3) team brief. Everything else is nice-to-have.
